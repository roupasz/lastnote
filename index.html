Why LastNote

Local

Nothing leaves your machine

Everything you write is stored and encrypted on your own device. No account required, no server in between.

Organized

One place for everything that matters

Accounts, instructions, documents, and notes to loved ones, kept together instead of scattered across apps and drawers.

Encrypted

Locked until it's needed

Strong encryption keeps every entry private, opened only with the key you choose.

Synchronized

Sync changes to Git & Cloud Storage

Add your Personal Access Token (PAT), keep track and history of your changes.
